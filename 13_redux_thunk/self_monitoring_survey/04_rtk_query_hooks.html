2. How does cache invalidation work in RTK Query?

(x) Queries declare providesTags and mutations declare invalidatesTags — when a mutation invalidates a tag, all queries providing that tag automatically refetch

3. What happens when a mutation with invalidatesTags: ["Post"] succeeds?
